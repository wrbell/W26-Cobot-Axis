Course Design Project Phase 2: Design and Preliminary Analysis

Design your idea project on paper (or electronically) and perform the requisite engineering analyses to support your design. You should be able to do this without using computational engineering analysis such as CFD, FEM, and multiphysics solvers. Calculus, differential equations, linear algebra, and design reference tables should be enough. Using MATLAB is fine for an analytical numerical model. You will be consulting online catalogs and specification sheets.

Show sketches of your planned

block diagram of functions/signals,
circuit diagram,
circuit layout, and
mechanical components.
Submit a PDF (5 pages or less) of your preliminary design, bill of materials, and analysis as your midterm project update. Again, the instructor responds with feedback to modify (perhaps simplify) the design and a go/no decision on advancing to the next phase. The instructor will make the purchases using the course budget unless you are planning to keep your project in which case you purchase the components.

Include a bill of materials for the design and specific purchasing instructions for the instructor to make the purchase from UMich contracted suppliers Digikey, Newark, Grainger, MSC Direct, and/or BH Photo Video. It is okay to have items from multiple UMich suppliers.

Indicate which components will need to be 3D printed and provide design sketches (by hand or CAD) for those components. The instructor has 3D printing capabilities available for you to use.
